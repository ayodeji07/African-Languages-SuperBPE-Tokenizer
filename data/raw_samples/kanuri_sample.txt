1 Korintiwu < 0 > ^ 1 Korintiwu LEWA-A ALAA WUSEDU-A JAAWU BULUSSE KƎLA AWO FANJƎNADƎYEN Yaktu jama Isaa gawuye Almasiwudǝ nanrashidu Alaye Kǝska karmoye Almasiwuye mawodu Bayin Ruhu Alaye Sawawa kida Alaye Kida Kǝngayambaye Kam diwidǝa dawundon tuluwowo! Bare kǝramindoa fuwu sharayero yaduwwi! Tiyindolan Alaro daraja yowo! JAAWU BULUSSE KƎLA AWO TIRO RUWUJAANADƎYEN Kǝla larusa so, niya soyen Wundu yaye naptunjun dǝga! Kǝla am kamu cadanni-a kamuwa kwa faccaanaa-ayen Kǝla da sǝnǝmbo tinayen Awo kǝngayambaro amardǝgǝna Misal kǝla tuɍu Israyilawuyen Kǝla sǝnǝm gayen Awin yaye daraja Alaro tio! Kǝla Alaro ambǝttuwu-a kǝla jakta-ayen Cammonja ngǝla gǝni Masǝna Kǝmaye Kǝla kǝnjowa Ruhu Alayen Tiyidǝ falma, nasha gadegade kada Jawal kǝrawoye Kǝla nawinawi-a tǝlam jiɍi jiɍi-ayen Cammo jama Isaa gawuye sawiyada Karmolan cidu Almasiwuye Karmolan cidu am
